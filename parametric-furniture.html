Pseudo-Sentient

Pseudo-Sentient explores sculptural furniture imagined as if designed by entities with only a partial understanding of human needs. Their unfamiliar logic produces unusual structures, strange proportions, and forms that occasionally echo legs, roots, tentacles, or other natural forms.
